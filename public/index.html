New session

Steps — targets are % of your FTP. Fill “to” to make a ramp, leave it empty for a flat step. Fill “Cadence” (e.g. 55-65) for a low- or high-cadence step; Freehub shows the target and tells you to speed up or slow down. Fill “Hold HR” and the app adjusts your power to keep that heart rate (From % is the starting power).

Connect Strava

Optional. Lets Freehub send your rides to Strava. It takes about 3 minutes, once. You create your own Strava app, so your keys belong to you.
  1. Open your Strava API page.
    Sign in if asked. If you don't have an app yet, Strava shows a form to create one.
  2. Fill in the form. Application name: Freehub (anything works). Category: Training. Website: any address, for example https://example.com. Description: anything.
    Authorization Callback Domain must be exactly localhost
    No http://, no slashes, no port. If Strava ever shows redirect_uri invalid, this domain doesn't match what is saved on your Strava app. If Strava asks for an icon, any picture will do. Then save.
  3. Copy your two keys from that page: the Client ID (a number) and the Client Secret (click "Show" next to it), and paste them below.
Saved keys are for Strava app . If Strava shows redirect_uri invalid, or this is the wrong app, paste the right keys above, or and start again.

Your data

Freehub is running in your browser. Your rides, plans and routes are stored on this device only, and clearing your browser data deletes them. Download a backup now and then; you can restore it on any device.
