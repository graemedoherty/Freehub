# Trying Freehub (for testers)

Freehub is a free indoor-cycling dashboard for a smart trainer (such as a Zwift Hub). It shows your power, cadence, speed and heart rate, runs structured workouts, lets you ride a real route from a GPX file, and keeps your history.

## What you need
- A computer or Android phone with **Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge**. Safari, Firefox and iPhones can't connect to a trainer over Bluetooth.
- A smart trainer that supports Bluetooth FTMS (a Zwift Hub does). Close the Zwift or Companion app first, because a trainer accepts only one connection.
- Optional: a Bluetooth heart rate strap.

## Start
1. Open the Freehub website (the link I sent you) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click **Connect Zwift Hub** and pick your trainer. Click **Connect HR strap** if you have one.
3. Click **Start** and ride. Click **Stop** when you finish.

## Where is my data?
In your browser, on that device only. There are no accounts. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so click **Data & help → Download backup** now and then. You can restore the backup on another device.

## Strava (optional)
Click **Set up Strava** and follow the three steps. You create your own free Strava app, so your keys stay yours. Nothing is shared with me. If it doesn't work in your browser, download the ride's .tcx file from **History** and upload it at strava.com/upload/select.
